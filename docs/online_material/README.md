# Online material

Online material for the manuscript "Beyond screening: the tuberculosis–diabetes care cascade across India's States, 2018–2023". Every file here is generated by `exhibits/make_online_material.py` from the committed `outputs/` (Table S1 from the committed extraction in `data/extracted/`), and checked by `verification/check_online_material.py`. All values are aggregates of published State and Union Territory tables; the study holds no individual-level data.

## Notation

The manuscript describes these quantities in words; the tables use the symbols.

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| C1 | screening coverage: people notified with TB who had a known diabetes status, among those notified |
| y | the share identified: diabetes recorded (known or newly found) among those screened; not a new-detection yield |
| p_u | the reference prevalence of unit u: the NFHS-5 (2019-21) share of adults aged 15 years and over with a random blood glucose above 140 mg/dl or taking medicine for it, weighted by the female share of people notified with TB |
| k_b | the benchmark multiplier, 0.822: the 75th percentile across units of the shrunken share identified relative to p_u (2021 and 2022 pooled); a relative, peer benchmark |
| C2 | the identification ratio, y / (k_b p_u); below the benchmark when P(C2 < 1) is at least 0.95 |
| C3 | linkage: started on anti-diabetic treatment among those identified (2018: linked to an NCD clinic); may understate treatment, because people already on treatment may not be recorded as initiated |
| E1 | the screening gap: people with TB and diabetes expected among those not screened |
| E2 | the identification gap: expected but not identified among those screened, floored at zero (so a unit at or above the benchmark has none, and is screening-led by definition) |
| E3 | the linkage gap: identified but not started on treatment |
| P(top-5) | the probability of being among the five units with the largest gap; 0 or 1 for every unit in 2023, so the act-now tier is the five largest absolute gaps |
| H1 | the registered hypothesis: the slope of identification on the logit of p_u (labelled supported) |
| H2, H3 | registered descriptive results: the change in national screening coverage 2018 to 2022, and the number of units below the benchmark in 2023 |

TB, tuberculosis; NFHS-5, National Family Health Survey 2019-21; NCD, non-communicable disease; UT, Union Territory.
